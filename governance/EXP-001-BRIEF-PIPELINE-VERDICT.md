# EXP-001 — Brief-Driven Pipeline vs One-Pass Writing: VERDICT (CANON)

**Status:** Canon evidence record. Locked by Marc, 2026-07-19 (Telegram msg 2982).
**Experiment:** EXP-BRIEF-VS-DIRECT-001 ("Empty Weight" premise, author Julian Mercer). Rulings: Marc msgs 2826 (2026-07-17) + 2982 (2026-07-19).

## Design
Same premise written two ways, scored blind (headers stripped, randomized labels, single scoring pass, Sonnet-class scorer whose entire context was the approved rubric + both scripts):
- **ARM A:** one-pass direct write. No brief, no NEDS gate, no pipeline discipline.
- **ARM B:** full pipeline — BRIEF v2.2 → NEDS gate (9/9) → Stage 2 script generation.
Rubric: canonical 30-point (Master Bible §4), Option B adaptation (Audio dimension → Production Readiness text proxy), gates publish ≥22 / gold ≥26, plus hard gates. Approved by Marc before scoring.

## Result
| Arm | Score | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| ARM B (pipeline) | **29/30** | **GOLD** — clears publish and gold gates |
| ARM A (one-pass) | 22/30 | **DIAGNOSE** — hard-gate failures override the numeric total: G1 (no resolution map) + G4 (climax off-page/secondhand) |

Largest deltas (B−A): **Production Readiness +3** (A: 12 SFX vs 6-max canon, parentheticals in dialogue, ambience beds; B: exactly 6 discrete anchors, one paired music moment, zero parentheticals) and **Hook +2** (B hooks inside 15 words; A spends 60–90s on setting/backstory).

## Canon consequence (Marc, msg 2982)
1. **The brief/NEDS pipeline is mandatory for story production.** This experiment is the recorded evidence: the pipeline beat one-pass writing precisely on the dimensions the pipeline exists to enforce (structure gates, hook discipline, production readiness).
2. EXP-001 is closed. No ear-test; audio hold permanent. Totals live in the experiment log only, never the Gold Standard log.
3. ARM A's script is cold-storaged; its premise is freed per PREMISE-UNIQUENESS-001 cold-storage exemption. "Empty Weight" may deserve a real pipeline run via an ARM B-style brief (post-launch queue: EMPTY-WEIGHT-RERUN-001).
