**Ep1 (promo + library):** Suitcase under Liberty Bridge surfaces. Claire traces it to a burner phone — one contact — leading her to Locker 214 at Greenville's old train terminal. Lights go dark. A woman's voice through the headphones: "Claire, they told everyone we disappeared… We didn't." [CLIFFHANGER]

**Ep2:** The voice is Diana Voss — forensic accountant, missing 90 days. She's alive and hiding. She tells Claire: the three women (city planner Renata Osei, Falls Park ranger Mara Fields, photographer Diane Cho) weren't taken. They were coerced into silence by a private security firm working for City Councilman Dale Merritt — held in a converted riverside property while a billion-dollar Reedy River rezoning vote approaches.

**Ep3:** Claire digs into the rezoning — Merritt's associates stand to control the entire river corridor. Each missing woman held a piece of evidence that could kill the vote. Claire finds Mara's hidden field notes in Falls Park, confirming the land-use fraud.

**Ep4:** Someone tampers with Claire's car near Liberty Bridge. A trusted police contact is feeding her movements to Merritt's team. Claire goes off-grid, communicating only through Diana. The vote is six days out.

**Ep5:** Claire locates the warehouse where the women are held — legally permitted under Merritt's own rezoning. The women refuse to leave: their families have been threatened. Claire needs leverage strong enough to break the hold.

**Ep6:** Diana goes dark for 18 hours. Claire finds a second locker — Diana's failsafe: a hard drive with financial records, communications, and a full list of compromised officials. Merritt's people corner Claire before she can act.

**Ep7 (FINALE):** Claire goes live on air and reads from the hard drive before it can be suppressed. FBI — already tracking Merritt on a separate matter — moves immediately. The three women are released. Merritt and his network are arrested. Diana Voss steps out of hiding and gives her statement on Liberty Bridge — the exact spot where the suitcase was found. The four women stand together. Claire signs off.
