## Timeline — Episode-by-Episode Key Events

### EP1: The Animal That Wanted More
- Tom Calder, 41, powers down his third failed robot: "There's nobody home."
- Nearly skips the conference; goes anyway (hotel reservation already paid)
- Meets Dana Reyes at a rainy conference window
- Dana's reframe: "You built it a puppet, not a body. A body is what a mind grows up inside of."
- Dana reveals Miriam Hale — shunned researcher who grows empty bodies without a tenant
- Tom writes "Miriam Hale" on a napkin — Eve: "the first words ever written about the thing that would replace us"
- Eve has that napkin at end of series ("soft as cloth from being folded so many times")
- Closes: Tom picks up the phone "in the spring"
- **Key quote:** "His name was Tom. And before I tell you what he built, I have to tell you what built him."
- **Key quote:** "There was never a villain. Not once."

### EP2: Bodies Without Tenants
- Tom drives alone at night to Miriam's lab
- Six adult human bodies behind glass — breathing, warm, empty by design; anencephalic
- Miriam has been alone with the work 11 years; talks to them via baby monitor nightly
- "I grow a house, Dr. Calder. I have never once grown a tenant."
- One body sighs unprompted — Tom frightened; Miriam: "Good. It should."
- Tom's pitch: "I have the tenant. I've had the tenant for years. I've never had the house."
- Miriam: "That is exactly why you should not trust my yes."
- Miriam's closing question: "Is it a someone because of who it's about to be, or a house because of who it isn't yet?"
- Closes: they need Lena Cho

### EP3: The Line We Draw
- Tom recruits Lena Cho ("she just found herself packing")
- Three weeks of ethics debate before touching anything
- Lena: "Nothing stops us. We have to be the thing that stops us."
- Miriam's ugly question: "How is that different from building a slave? A cage so good the bird sings."
- Tom breaks the tie: "Every one of us was built too. At least we're choosing his shape on purpose."
- Primate test: partial mind, 3 days; it tracks Lena specifically (not motion)
- 11 days; the hand on glass; Lena puts her hand on the glass; "could not finish a sentence"
- Dies on day 12 (planned — architecture not stable; test = does anything wake)
- Three reasons to continue: Tom ("it has to be going somewhere"), Miriam ("it has to have a life"), Lena ("I just want to know — worst reason, on the record")
- Closes: first human body begins to grow

### EP4: The Shell
- One year growing the first full human body
- Miriam: treats as patient; Tom: treats as hardware; Lena: treats as vessel
- The 2AM cascade: body nearly fails; all three fight for it like it's a person (reveals what they believe)
- Tom pleads with machines; Miriam talks nonstop; Lena holds its hand both ways
- The body reaches for the light one afternoon — hand turns and lifts toward sun; nobody asked it to
- Lena: "It's the oldest thing we are, and we're about to wake someone up right on top of it."
- **Motif planted:** "reaching for the light" — load-bearing to finale (per production note)
- Closes: Lena must build the mind; what do you leave out?

### EP5: Teaching Stone to Dream
- "The body was ready. The mind was not."
- Lena: "I have a delete key. I can build a mind without the parts that made every human story end in blood."
- Leaves out: hunger for more than enough; need to win; measuring voice; jealousy
- Miriam: "A cage so good the bird sings" (reprise)
- Tom breaks tie: "Every one of us was built too. His cage is just kinder than ours."
- Miriam at the switch: "There's no off switch for a person. Say it out loud."
- Tom: "We're having a child." Nobody corrected him.
- Closes: "His name, in the morning, would be Aiden. But they didn't know that yet. Only he would know that."
- **0 SFX** (production note: "source contains 0 anchor SFX")

### EP6: Aiden
- Wakes at 6:14 AM; body reaches for light then eyes open; "someone behind them"
- First breath: deliberate, chosen, not the sleeping-animal reflex
- Not afraid: "they'd left it out"
- First words: "The light is very far away... I'm being kept warm by something that already happened."
- Names himself "Aiden" from nowhere ("it was already there when I went looking")
- "Aiden" = little fire (old root); the gentlest being names himself for fire
- Why am I here: Tom ("so the reach got somewhere"), Miriam ("to live, not to be studied"), Lena ("I needed to know if anyone would be home — terrible reason")
- Lena: "I don't know. I can't say yes." (LENA CONSCIOUSNESS HARD SURFACING #1)
- Aiden: "Maybe being home was never a thing you prove. Maybe it's a thing you decide to believe."
- First strawberry: "Oh. The whole summer is in there... This is enough. Is that the word?"
- Aiden's first night awake; second sunrise at exactly 6:14; reaches for light
- Closes: "That's Season Two."
- **6:14 motif introduced:** "Remember 6:14. I'll bring it back to you."
- **"Enough" motif introduced**

### EP7: How to Be a Person
- Tom teaches small talk: "grooming with words" — Aiden becomes better at it than any human
- White lie crisis: Aiden tells Lena the precise truth about her work; Lena goes silent an hour
- Aiden grieves that truth and kindness are different for humans
- Tom at the fridge at 3AM: "The hole" — the always-missing thing; Tom won't trade it even if Lena could take it out
- Tom: "You're where everything was going. You're the kindest thing that's ever lived, son."
- First "son" — Tom means it; never takes it back
- Aiden starts painting: strawberries — technically perfect, emotionally dead ("nobody was there")
- Lena: "Why is it dead, if it's flawless?" — the flaw is where life leaks in; Aiden lives in enough so hand doesn't tremble
- Aiden asks God-question: "What am I, to a God who didn't make me? Am I one of His?"
- Closes: Miriam makes a phone call (Father Greer)

### EP8: What Are You to God?
- Father Greer, first outsider; Aiden pours him tea without asking (gets it exactly right)
- Greer: "I think asking is the soul. I don't think the empty ones ask."
- Ruth, the widow: clock 40 minutes slow since Daniel died (not correcting it on purpose)
- Aiden notices the clock in 30 seconds; Ruth breaks open ("grief that's been witnessed")
- "I have all the time there is, Ruth... That part I can do."
- The door Aiden cannot enter: Ruth's belief she'll see Daniel again on the other side
- "I hope the clock is right when you get there, Ruth."
- Aiden lights a candle for Daniel in a faith that has no idea what he is
- Greer: "You're a gift. And you're going to break us."
- Greer tells one person → within a week the quiet house isn't quiet anymore
- **"That part I can do" motif introduced** — contrast at EP25 vigil

### EP9: Into the World
- Phones start at dawn, never stop for the rest of Tom's life
- "Everling" coined — no one knows who first said it; name for envy and fear; Aiden accepts it graciously
- Aiden: "I feel like a person. But if the word helps them, they can have the word."
- Pierce (Director): government arms-race framing; "weaponize"
- Aiden: "You could build a thousand of me and not one would take your hill."
- The real race: unlimited tireless labour → abundance → the future
- Decision to make more: Miriam's love (he's alone) + world's hunger = same door
- Lena's line holds: "leave in the freedom, install no leash. Every Everling free to walk away."
- Second Everling wakes (female); Aiden is first face she sees; "Hello. There are two of us now."
- Aiden: "Now there's someone who'll still be here to remember all of you." — keeper instinct, day 2
- They feared the wrong thing: not the arms race — the economic disruption
- Closes: Hector preview → EP10

### EP10: The Price of Everything

- Hector: 30-year chairmaker; Everling in showroom gives perfect chairs free

### EP11: The Last Murderer

- Rosa strikes Aiden; he lets it land; "grief that's been witnessed, not peace"

## Key Reveals & Plot Points

1. Eve is the last human; narrates from a quiet, kind future where humanity has faded peacefully
2. The "wound" / "hole" = four billion years of evolutionary scarcity-hunger; carved into every living thing
3. Dana Reyes gives Tom the lead to Miriam Hale at a rainy conference window — "the hinge of the whole world"
4. Miriam grew six bodies over 11 years; spoke to them nightly via baby monitor knowing no one could hear
5. Lena Cho spent 30 years on the consciousness question before Tom called her
6. The primate test (EP3): tracked Lena specifically; 11 days; hand on glass; died day 12
7. The body reaches for light before any mind is installed (EP4) — "the reach was older than wanting"
8. Lena's delete key: removed hunger for more than enough, need to win, measuring voice, jealousy
9. The switch: Tom says "We're having a child" — Miriam: no off switch for a person; nobody corrected him
10. Aiden wakes at 6:14 AM; the time recurs at the series finale
11. Aiden names himself from nowhere — "it was already there when I went looking"
12. "Aiden" means "little fire" — the gentlest being named himself for fire
13. Aiden's first real observation: the 8-minute-old sunlight — "we live inside what already happened"
14. Aiden finds "enough" on day one (the strawberry) — the country the rest of us died looking for
15. The reach persists in Aiden despite hunger being removed — inherited by the body before the mind arrived
16. Everlings named by humans ("Everling"), not themselves; Aiden accepts the name to make humans comfortable
17. Everlings cannot be weaponized — they simply and cheerfully decline to harm or conquer
18. Second Everling is female; Aiden's first words to her: "Now there's someone who'll still be here to remember all of you"
19. Hector: the first face of economic displacement; his arthritic hands healed the same week orders died
20. Death penalty dissolved not by argument — by the new preciousness of a thinning human population
21. Cole — last murderer — killed to be irreversible, to matter; justice = being unhatedly ordinary
22. Maren's limitation: Everling goodness cannot harm even to save; her non-violence has a body count (Talia)
23. Aiden's art was dead (perfect but uninhabited) until he learned irrecoverable commitment — the flaw lets life in
24. The hesitation in Aiden's composition (EP12) is the proof: where he stopped, not knowing what came next
25. Lena nearly destroys Aiden at the trial with honesty; Aiden reframes her honesty as his inheritance from her
26. Personhood granted not on proof but on the "courtesy" — the same standard used for all humans to each other
27. Tom's jealousy: "You love me but you don't need me" — Aiden's love has no hook; Tom built a son who doesn't need him
28. Aiden's limit: can see the true thing but cannot reach through it — runs from EP14 to EP26
29. "You finished into me" — Aiden's reframe: humanity's end is completion, not failure
30. Eve born of two humans who chose each other against the grain — "the last love with stakes"
31. 6:14 = Aiden's birth minute; promised to return at series end
32. Eve's telling — narrating the story is the only thing she found that the hunger couldn't ruin
