## SAMPLE LINE TEXT

**NARRATOR (June Harlow) — real Bell EP1 opening:**
> "The maintenance door beneath Liberty Bridge did not appear on any city plan. Mara Vance had spent three days looking at city plans. She had pulled infrastructure maps from three different decades of the Greenville filing system — the river easements, the mill corridor drawings, the updated utility overlays. The door was not on any of them."

**MARA VANCE:**
> "The tunnel smelled of wet brick and old iron. Adrian lay beside the water channel, one hand wrapped around a brass token…"

**CLAIRE VANCE (cassette-treated):**

**DETECTIVE LENA ORTIZ:**
> "The edge is engraved. It says, 'Room two seventeen.'"

**ADRIAN CROSS (dying — stability 0.25, speaker_boost off):**

**JUNE BELL (through phone, ATL-HEADPHONE-001 filter):**

**UNKNOWN MAN (through phone, ATL-HEADPHONE-001 filter):**
