## ITEM 1 — Adrian's position and token location

**CONFLICT: YES (hard)**

PV1 audio says: *"Adrian lay beside the water channel, one hand wrapped around a brass token, stamped with a bell"*

PV2 audio says: *"found him face-down beside the runoff channel"* / *"Adrian's fist held a brass mill token, stamped with a bell"*

EP1 script says: *"He was pressed against the far wall with his head tilted slightly... ADRIAN: Left pocket. Take what's there."*

**Gap:**
- PV1/PV2 = beside the water/runoff channel; token in hand. EP1 = against far wall; token in left pocket.
- Both promos place Adrian near the water. EP1 places him against the wall. PV3 (canon) says "riverbank under Liberty Bridge → Adrian" — revised EP1 should match PV3/PV1/PV2: Adrian near water, token from hand.

---

## ITEM 2 — Lena's first appearance

**CONFLICT: YES (hard)**

PV1 audio says: *"Detective Lena Ortiz crouched over him"* (crouching over Adrian's body)

PV2 audio says: *"Detective Lena Ortiz appeared from the opposite passage, already gloved"* (entering dynamically from an adjacent tunnel)

EP1 script says: *"She had been there long enough that she looked like part of the tunnel. LENA: Mara Vance. I was starting to wonder."* (already in position, waiting before Mara found Adrian; directs Mara with "He's further in")

**Gap:** PV1 has Lena at Adrian's body; PV2 has her entering from a passage. EP1 has her pre-positioned in the tunnel before Mara arrives. These are three different staging choices for the same character introduction.

## ITEM 3 — Palm inscription

**CONFLICT: YES (hard)**

PV1 audio says: *"Adrian's dead fingers loosened, inside his palm, written in fresh black ink, were three words. Ask who drowned."*

PV2 audio says: *"Adrian's hand opened. Across his palm in black ink, someone had written, ask who drowned. Beneath the words was a date. June 18th, 1997."*

EP1 script says: Adrian says *"The bell knows."* and goes still. **No palm inscription anywhere in EP1.**

**Gap:**
- Both PV1 and PV2 have "ask who drowned" in black ink on Adrian's palm. PV2 also adds "June 18th, 1997" as the date.
- EP1 has no palm inscription; Adrian's final words are "The bell knows."
- Note: June 18, 1997 is canon (established in MEMORY.md), but this date appears on the palm in PV1/PV2, not in EP1 at all.
- When EP1 is revised to absorb PV3, this needs a resolution: does the palm inscription appear? PV3 has neither "ask who drowned" nor palm inscription.

## ITEM 4 — Adrian's apparent state

**CONFLICT: YES (moderate → note on framing)**

PV1 audio says: *"He has no pulse, Mara stepped back. Then the dead man's eyes opened"* / *"His chest did not move. His skin was already cold."*

EP1 script says: *"He was still breathing."* Adrian is clearly alive and speaks multiple sentences.

**Gap:** PV1 uses a "appears dead, then speaks" conceit (no pulse, cold skin, then eyes open). EP1 has Adrian clearly alive, pressed against wall, making eye contact and giving Mara instructions. The "dead man whispers" framing is in PV1 but EP1 doesn't use it. Not necessarily a hard plot contradiction — PV1 is a promo using heightened dramatic framing. But if EP1 is meant to be an excerpt of PV1, these are inconsistent.

## ITEM 5 — Bell rings physically in tunnel

**CONFLICT: YES (moderate)**

PV1 audio says: *"a bell rang somewhere far below us, deeper than the tunnel itself. There is no bell under Falls Park."*

EP1 script says: Adrian says *"The bell knows."* No SFX bell ringing. The bell does not physically ring as an audible event in EP1.

**Gap:** PV1 has the bell physically ringing in the tunnel (off-screen, below them). EP1 has no such SFX — only Adrian's final words reference the bell. This is a significant atmospheric detail PV1 creates that EP1 does not deliver.

## ITEM 6 — Entry to hidden room

**CONFLICT: YES (hard)**

PV2 audio says: *"a fresh arrow pointed toward a narrow iron door... That door should open into solid earth. It opened into a hidden room."*

EP1 script says: *"Until Mara pressed her hand flat against the stone and found the seam. [SFX: Stone grinding slowly as a concealed panel shifts in the tunnel wall, revealing a gap in the passage]"*

**Gap:** PV2 = a narrow IRON DOOR that opens physically. EP1 = a concealed panel (seam in wall) discovered by touch. Two different entry mechanisms. PV3 doesn't specify an entry mechanism; revised EP1 should choose one.

Also: PV2 says the wall is *"scratched with names"* (plural, multiple names). EP1 has only Claire Vance's name carved in the wall.

## ITEM 7 — Cassette trigger

**CONFLICT: YES (moderate)**

PV2 audio says: *"The cassette clicked on by itself."*

EP1 script says: *"She pressed play."*

PV3 B2 audio says: *"I pressed the play button."*

**Gap:** PV2 = cassette auto-activates. EP1 and PV3 = Mara presses play manually. PV3 is canon → EP1 is correct here. PV2's auto-activation is the outlier.

## ITEM 8 — Lena shouts before gunshot

**CONFLICT: YES (moderate)**

PV2 audio says: *"From the tunnel, Lena shouted that she had caught the runner. A second later came a gunshot."*

EP1 script says: *"LENA: Stay here. Both of you. She was gone. [Running footsteps...] [A single gunshot — muffled by stone and distance]"* — no communication from Lena before the shot.

**Gap:** PV2 has Lena communicating back (confirming she caught the runner) before the gun fires. EP1 has silence, then shot. When EP1 is revised to absorb PV3 (gunshot in hotel hallway), this conflict may resolve or evolve.

## ITEM 9 — Room contents

**CONFLICT: YES (moderate)**

PV2 audio says: *"Inside stood a wooden chair, a cassette recorder, and five photographs pinned beneath a city flood map."*

EP1 script says: *"A wooden chair. A cassette recorder on the floor beside it, gray with age but solid. On the far wall, five photographs pinned in a single careful row."* (no flood map; also has a key taped behind Eli's photograph, not in PV2)

PV3 B2 audio says: *"The windowless room held a folding table, a cassette recorder, a Greenville street map, and six brass bell tokens arranged in a circle."* (no photographs; six tokens)

**Gap:**
- PV2: chair, cassette, 5 photos beneath flood map
- EP1: chair, cassette, 5 photos + key behind Eli's photo (no map)
- PV3 (canon): folding table, cassette, street map, six tokens (no photos)
- All three versions differ. Marc's ruling: EP1 absorbs PV3 sequence; PV3 room (table, cassette, map, six tokens) becomes the Palmetto Hotel room; EP1's hidden tunnel room (photos, key behind Eli's photo) stays as a LATER beat.
