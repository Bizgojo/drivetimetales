## === EPISODE 2 EXPANDED — "Nobody Saw Her Leave" ===

SFX: The back door of the coffee shop clicks shut. Refrigerator hum. Footsteps on tile.
DENNY: Tell me we're not doing this.
JAMIE: We already did it.
DENNY: I mean tell me we're not about to do what I think comes next.
JAMIE: Go get the bag. It's still under the counter.
DENNY: Jamie. Priya moved. She texted people when she got to Charlotte.
JAMIE: Did you see the text yourself?
DENNY: She told me she was going. Before she left.
JAMIE: Did you see her get in the truck?
DENNY: She said goodbye.
JAMIE: To you directly?
DENNY: (a pause) In the group chat.
JAMIE: Nobody saw her leave, Denny. Not one person.
JAMIE (V.O.): Six weeks ago, everyone said Priya packed a U-Haul and left for Charlotte. Nobody actually saw her go.
DENNY: You really kept the badge.
JAMIE: I kept the whole bag. It's got her phone in it too. Dead, but it's charging now.
DENNY: This feels like something we hand to the police, not something we plug into a wall outlet.
JAMIE: And tell them what — I broke into a wall that doesn't exist and found a bag that isn't mine?
DENNY: Fair.
SFX: The phone chimes softly. Screen glow.
DENNY: You know there's a rational explanation for all of this.
JAMIE: Tell me.
DENNY: Building crew. They've been in that block all month. Someone's using the tunnel as a break space—
JAMIE: The cot was military fold. Tight corners. That's not a construction worker on lunch.
DENNY: People fold differently—
JAMIE: Red Bull. Six cans, same brand, same flavor. Someone with a routine. Not someone who wandered in.
DENNY: (quieter) Okay.
JAMIE: The extension cord ran up the wall through a hole cut clean. That hole was there before the renovation. And it was powered, Denny. I touched it. It hummed.
DENNY: (quietly) Okay. I hear you.
JAMIE: It's on. No lock code.
DENNY: That's not good. That's the opposite of good.
JAMIE: Texts stop six weeks ago. Same day everyone says she "left."
SFX: A tap on the screen. The recording begins — thin, phone quality.
PRIYA (RECORDING): (whispering, scared) If anyone finds this — I found a second door. Down past the—
SFX: Static. Then silence.
JAMIE: That's it. That's the whole file.
JAMIE (V.O.): Twenty-two seconds. And then nothing.
DENNY: Play it again.
SFX: The recording plays again.
JAMIE: "A second door." Past the what? It cuts off right before—
DENNY: Right before whatever mattered. Yeah. Of course it does.
JAMIE: One door in the alley. She's saying there's another one, somewhere past that.
DENNY: Or she's saying there's a door and then something grabbed her and we're never going to know what.
JAMIE: Don't say that.
DENNY: I'm not trying to be dark, Jamie, I'm trying to be the one person in this conversation with common sense.
JAMIE: Then use it. Help me figure out what she meant.
SFX: Slow soft scrolling. Taps.
JAMIE: The texts are gone but the photos aren't.
DENNY: How far back do they go?
JAMIE: Seven months. She took a lot of pictures of the block. Same angles, different times of day. Like she was tracking something.
DENNY: That's strange.
JAMIE: There are dates. She was systematic about it.
SFX: A pause. A single tap.
JAMIE: Denny.
DENNY: What?
JAMIE: Look at this.
SFX: Silence.
DENNY: (very low, very careful) Delete that.
JAMIE: What? Why—
DENNY: Right now. Delete it and we don't go back down there. We don't call anyone. We act like tonight didn't happen.
JAMIE: (shaken) You know what this means.
DENNY: Yes. Delete it, Jamie.
SFX: The sound of the phone being set on the counter. Not deleted.
JAMIE: Wait. There's a location icon. Green dot. Active.
DENNY: Active — like, right now?
JAMIE: Right now.
JAMIE: Denny. It's moving.
DENNY: What's moving?
JAMIE: The dot. Her phone's dot. It's not sitting still — it's moving, right now, underground, in a straight line down Main.
DENNY: That's not possible. The phone's sitting right here on the counter.
JAMIE: I know where the phone is, Denny. I'm looking at it. So who's carrying the other one?
DENNY: ...There's another phone.
JAMIE: Signed into the same account. Has to be.
SFX: Both of them watching the screen.
DENNY: Where is it going?
JAMIE: South. Under the block — past the antique store, past the gym—
DENNY: That's over a hundred yards underground.
JAMIE: Still moving. Consistent. Like someone who knows where they're going.
DENNY: (watching) It's slowing.
JAMIE: I see it.
DENNY: Okay. That settles it. We call someone tomorrow. A real someone. Police, campus security, I don't care who—
JAMIE: And say what — that a phone is moving underground? They'll laugh us out of the station.
DENNY: Better than being the two idiots who go back down that tunnel a second time.
JAMIE: It stopped.
DENNY: What?
JAMIE: The dot. It stopped moving. Right under—
JAMIE: It stopped right under this building. Right under where we're standing.
SFX: Silence. Neither of them moves.
DENNY: (barely audible) How long has it been there?
JAMIE: (watching the screen) Forty seconds. It's not moving.
DENNY: Is it—
JAMIE: Still. Still and waiting.
SFX: Jamie lowering herself slowly to the floor. Pressing her ear to the old hardwood.
DENNY: (barely audible) What are you doing?
JAMIE (V.O.): The floor in the back of the shop is old hardwood laid over concrete. But in the middle, where the pipes run, it's hollow if you know how to listen.
SFX: A long held silence. Then — three slow, deliberate knocks from below.
DENNY: Tell me you heard that too.
SFX: Silence. A moment. Then the knocks again — closer, louder, two more.
JAMIE: Don't move.
DENNY: (shaking) Something is knocking from under the floor of our coffee shop.
JAMIE (V.O.): Six weeks ago, Priya vanished from a tunnel nobody was supposed to know about. Tonight, something in that same tunnel knew exactly where we were standing.
SFX: One final knock. Then silence — worse than the sound itself.
JAMIE: We're not calling the police tomorrow.
DENNY: We're not?
JAMIE: We're finding that second door. Tonight.
SFX: Music sting. Hard cut to black.

## === EPISODE 3 EXPANDED — "The Second Door" ===

SFX: A flashlight clicks on. Metal groans — the alley door.
JAMIE (V.O.): Two nights ago, I promised myself I'd never go back down there. Tonight, I was standing at the door again, and this time Denny wasn't trying to stop me.
DENNY: If we find a second door, we look. We don't open it. We look, and we leave, and we call someone with a badge that isn't stolen from a tunnel.
JAMIE: Deal.
SFX: Footsteps shift from gravel to hollow concrete. The outside noise fades.
JAMIE: Same tunnel. Same cans, same cot. Cable's still running.
SFX: A few more steps. Her light sweeps wider.
JAMIE: Wait. No.
DENNY: What?
JAMIE: The cord's gone.
DENNY: (looking) The cot's gone too.
JAMIE: Every can. Gone. Somebody cleared this place out.
DENNY: Tonight.
JAMIE: In the last two hours.
DENNY: (very quiet) Which means they know we were here.
JAMIE: (not looking away from the dark ahead) Yeah.
DENNY: Which means they know what we look like.
SFX: A long beat. Both of them standing still. Then Denny speaks quietly.
DENNY: Which way did the dot stop?
JAMIE: Past where I found the bag. Deeper.
SFX: Footsteps again. Slower. More careful.
JAMIE: Denny. Look at the wall.
DENNY: That's not brick. That's a door. Painted to look like the wall, but that's a door.
JAMIE (V.O.): The second door. Exactly where Priya's recording cut off.
DENNY: Okay. We look, like we said. We don't open it.
JAMIE: It's not locked.
DENNY: That doesn't mean open it.
JAMIE: Denny, she said "past the second door" and then the recording died. What if she didn't get to finish because someone stopped her — not because there was nothing left to say?
DENNY: You already have your hand on it, don't you.
JAMIE: ...Maybe.
SFX: The door swings inward. A low electrical hum.
JAMIE: This isn't a tunnel anymore. It's a room.
SFX: Their lights sweep a wider space — cables, a folding table, shelves.
DENNY: Jamie. Those are photos on the table.
JAMIE: Photos of...
SFX: She steps closer. Stops cold.
JAMIE: That's me. That's a photo of me. Leaving the shop last week.
DENNY: We're done looking. We're leaving. Now.
JAMIE: There's more. Other people. I don't recognize— wait. Priya. There's a whole folder with her name on it.
DENNY: Jamie—
SFX: She picks it up. Her light sweeps the back wall. Something there.
JAMIE: There's a grid. On the wall. Names and dates — it goes back months.
DENNY: Put it down.
JAMIE: There's a schedule. Same people, same blocks, same windows of time. Someone's been tracking—
DENNY: Jamie. There's another folder.
SFX: He picks it up carefully.
JAMIE: Who's in it?
DENNY: (reading, quiet) They're all women. All on Main Street. All with the same timeline.
JAMIE: (steady, cold) That's not surveillance. That's a list.
DENNY: Jamie—
JAMIE: We need proof. This is proof.
UNKNOWN VOICE: (closer than before, flat) You need to put that back. Right now.
DENNY: GO—
SFX: Running. Ragged breathing. Tunnel walls amplify every footstep.
JAMIE (V.O.): We ran the way we came. Except this time, the tunnel didn't feel the same length.
DENNY: Where's the door — the one we came in through—
JAMIE: It should be right— it should be right here—
SFX: Footsteps stop. Just their breathing.
DENNY: (quiet, terrified) Jamie. There's no door here. Just wall.
SFX: Somewhere behind them, back in the dark — a door opens. Then stops. No footsteps follow.
JAMIE (V.O.): They could have come after us. They chose not to. I don't know which part of that frightens me more.
JAMIE: (low, urgent) Give me the light. There has to be a seam, a hinge, something—
SFX: Her hands scraping along stone. Then — a metallic click. Something catches.
JAMIE: (relief flooding in) Here. It's here—
SFX: The footsteps start again behind them. Slow. Deliberate. Closing.
UNKNOWN VOICE: You shouldn't have come back.
JAMIE (V.O.): I shoved the door open and pulled Denny through, and for one second, before it slammed shut behind us, I saw something I still can't explain.
JAMIE: (shaking) Did you see that? On the ground, right before the door closed—
DENNY: (pale) A shoe. That was Priya's shoe.
SFX: Alley air. Distant traffic. Both of them breathing hard.
DENNY: (slowly) We're going to the police. Tonight. Right now.
JAMIE: Look at this.
SFX: She opens the folder she's still holding.
DENNY: Jamie, we need to go—
JAMIE: (quiet, flat) There's a name in here I know.
SFX: Silence.
DENNY: (doesn't ask)
JAMIE: (doesn't say it)
SFX: Traffic hums past, indifferent. The alley holds still.
JAMIE (V.O.): Priya didn't just find a door. She found what was behind it. And the people who built that room — who made that list — they knew she found it. I keep telling myself she got out. That the shoe was old. That there's still a version of this where she's in Charlotte with her phone off, just quiet. But I'm holding a folder with names I recognize, and her name is in it. And the date next to it is yesterday.
SFX: Music sting. Hard cut to black.
