ELI: That is a Camperdown night token. Workers carried them during flood watch along the Reedy River. Each one authenticated a checkpoint on the flood-watch route. The mill's bell warned when the river was rising faster than the gates could handle. The workers walked from checkpoint to checkpoint and carried the tokens to prove they'd been there.

LENA: What kind of checkpoints.

ELI: Drainage points. Overflow chambers. Gates. The infrastructure beneath the mill and along the river corridor. The entire system fed down through this tunnel network.

NARRATOR: Mara turned the disc over. The tilted striker stared up at her.

MARA: This one is different. The clapper is wrong.

ELI: Yes.

[BEAT]

LENA: A body.

ELI: It was a burial record built into the flood system. The mill kept two ledgers. The official ledger recorded flood activity and gate status. The inverted-clapper entries recorded what the infrastructure had been used to conceal. What the river had claimed, officially. And what had been placed into the underground system and left there by other means.

NARRATOR: The tunnel felt smaller than it had sixty seconds ago. The sound of water dripping from the ceiling had changed in her ears — or the meaning of it had changed, which produced the same effect.

MARA: My mother had one of these.

ELI: She had the first one, according to Adrian's research. There are five more.

MARA: Six tokens total.

ELI: Six checkpoints. Six waypoints on a route through this tunnel system. Whatever is at the end of that route — whatever Claire was moving toward when she disappeared — that is what she had found.
