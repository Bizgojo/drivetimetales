## PART 3 — STORY CONTENT

**The Core Premise:**

Detective Ward's investigation into the three basilica deaths intensifies. Toxicology confirms all three were poisoned with oleander extract — a deliberate, premeditated method. David Torrence's background begins to unravel: he wasn't the upstanding businessman everyone thought. Father Matthias had been receiving threatening letters. The unknown woman is identified as Sarah Chen, a former parishioner who had accused the church of covering up abuse. 

As Ward interviews the community, the basilica's carefully maintained image cracks. The old fiddle player reveals that he'd seen the priest and David Torrence arguing outside the basilica weeks before. Monsignor Kelly admits to a secret — the church's finances are deeply troubled. And then Ward finds the letters: Father Matthias had been blackmailed, threatening to expose years of hidden misconduct.

The case spirals. Who killed whom? Was it revenge? Was it something darker? And who placed the bodies in the wine cellar as if performing some kind of ritual?

**The Setting:**

Asheville, North Carolina. The detective bureau. Parish records room. David Torrence's home office. Sarah Chen's apartment. The basilica sanctuary and offices. Streets of Asheville. Hospital records office. The old fiddle player's apartment above a downtown pawn shop.

**Target Runtime:** 16 minutes

**Characters:**

- DETECTIVE JAMES WARD — continues investigating, growing uneasy about what he's finding
- ROSA DELGADO — gives a statement, but also reveals she'd been approached by someone asking about the basilica's schedule
- BISHOP CROSS — defensive, concerned about the church's reputation, pushing Ward to close the case quietly
- MONSIGNOR KELLY — reveals the blackmail letters, admits to financial cover-ups
- OLD FIDDLE PLAYER — seen the priest and Torrence arguing; plays a fiddle tune that becomes a motif for the mystery
- DETECTIVE SARAH MILLS — Ward's partner, a voice of reason, pushes him to see the pattern
- CORONER — reveals oleander extract, comments on the deliberate placement of bodies
- CHURCH SECRETARY (voice only) — nervous, caught between loyalty to the church and the truth
- DAVID TORRENCE'S EX-WIFE — reveals he was abusive, had dark secrets, owed people money

**Specific Story Requirements:**

- This is the investigation deepening episode. Secrets should tumble out. But with each revelation, the mystery should get *deeper*, not simpler.
- The poison is oleander extract — commonly available, intentional, ritualistic.
- The old fiddle player should be memorable and important. His observations matter.
- Monsignor Kelly's confession about finances creates a motive.
- Sarah Chen's involvement as a previous accuser creates a tangle: was she victim or killer?
- End on a revelation that opens a new question: Detective Ward finds evidence that one of the bodies may not be what they seem, or that there's a fourth person involved.
- Music should escalate in dread as more secrets tumble out.
